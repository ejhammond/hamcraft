Releases

What changed in each version of the HamCraft pack. Most players just want the latest version from the Download tab — older versions are here if you ever need to roll back.

  1. v26.3.0

    OCTOBER 21, 2026 CURRENT RELEASE
    • Minecraft 26.3 — the pack and server are updated to the latest version.
    • Elytra Slot is replaced by Armored Elytra: combine an elytra and a chestplate on an anvil to wear both as one piece; split them back at a grindstone.
    • Stack Refill replaces Auto Refill — your hotbar still refills from your inventory automatically.
    • Durability Notifier replaces Anti Item Break: tools no longer stop at 1 durability — you get a ping and an on-screen warning below 10% instead.
    • MobHealthBar lives on — we ported it to 26.3, so your hearts bar is unchanged.
    • Removed No Chat Reports and Krypton.
    Client pack (.mrpack) Server pack (.mrpack)

  2. v26.2.8

    OCTOBER 12, 2026
    • Added a trash slot to your inventory to dispose of unwanted items.
    • Removed Silk Touch spawner pickup — spawners can't be mined and relocated anymore.
    • Added a free camera — press F8 to detach your camera and fly around to admire your builds.
    • Added the Copper Hopper — a hopper made of copper that filters items, so you can build compact sorters without a giant redstone setup.
    • Added Respackopts — Redstone Tweaks now has a settings screen (from the Resource Packs list), so you can fine-tune it or turn off the container signal panels.
    • Bundled two optional resource packs (off by default — turn them on in Options → Resource Packs): a jewel on enchanted tools tells Fortune from Silk Touch at a glance, and Redstone Tweaks makes redstone components easier to read.
    Client pack (.mrpack) Server pack (.mrpack)

  3. v26.2.7

    OCTOBER 5, 2026
    • Chop down whole trees at once — put the VeinMiner enchantment on an axe and set up your hotkey in the keybinds menu.
    • Mine spawners with a Silk Touch pickaxe and place them back with the mob type intact.
    • Your hotbar refills itself from your inventory when a stack runs out.
    • Tools stop working at 1 durability instead of breaking — you'll get a warning before they give out.
    • Bundles are easier to use: click items in and out, with a preview on hover.
    • New villagers roll their best enchanted book prices.
    • Shaders are bundled — press I in-game to turn on Complementary Reimagined.
    Client pack (.mrpack) Server pack (.mrpack)

  4. v26.2.6

    OCTOBER 4, 2026
    • The first HamCraft release: graves keep your items safe when you die, potions stack, creeper explosions don't wreck your builds, and a minimap helps you find your way back.
    • Villagers can be leashed with a lead — moving them around no longer needs boats and minecarts.
    Client pack (.mrpack) Server pack (.mrpack)
